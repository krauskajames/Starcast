What Starcast is

Starcast combines public weather forecasts, astronomy calculations and map data to estimate observing conditions. It is a hobby project offered free of charge, for personal and educational use.

Forecasts are estimates

Weather forecasts can be wrong, and some values (seeing, transparency, light pollution, aurora chances and target scores) are Starcast's own estimates. Starcast is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, and may be incomplete, out of date or unavailable at times. Don't rely on it for decisions about safety, travel or property.

Safety

  • Never look at the Sun through a telescope, finder, binoculars or camera without a certified full-aperture solar filter. Doing so can cause permanent eye damage in an instant.
  • Places suggested under "Where to go" come from public map data. Check opening hours, access rules, permits, road and weather conditions before you travel, and respect private property and park rules.
