Starcast

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Starcast is a free, non-commercial astronomy forecasting website made by James Krauska. This policy explains what information Starcast handles, why, and how you can remove it. Starcast has no advertising, does not sell or share personal information for marketing, and does not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Using Starcast without an account

Everything you enter (saved locations, telescope and camera details, your observing log, settings and forecast checks) is stored only in your own web browser on that device. It is not sent to Starcast. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Using Starcast with an account

If you choose to sign in with Google, Starcast receives the basic profile Google shares with every sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture. Starcast does not receive your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

While you are signed in, Starcast stores the following so it can sync between your devices:

  • your name, email address and profile picture link from Google, to show who is signed in;
  • your saved observing locations (name, coordinates, sky darkness, horizon heights);
  • your gear, interests, observing log and notes, forecast checks and display settings.

This information is stored with Supabase, Starcast's database and sign-in provider, on servers in the United States. Database security rules allow each person to read and change only their own data. Starcast does not use this information for any purpose other than running the features you use, and does not share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run Starcast.

Services that receive locations

To show forecasts and sky information, your browser contacts these services directly. They receive the coordinates of places you look at, along with your IP address, as with any website request:

ServiceWhat it's used for
Open-MeteoWeather forecasts, haze forecasts and place search
OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) and OSRMFinding parks and driving times for "Where to go"
Map tile providers you choose (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Esri, CARTO, NASA)Base map images for the area on screen
NOAA, NASA and CelesTrakAurora forecasts, images of the Sun and satellite orbits (no location is sent)

If you use My location, your browser asks for permission first, and the position is used only to centre the map and fetch forecasts. If you set up alerts, the settings you copy into your own GitHub repository are handled by GitHub and the notification service you choose (ntfy or email); Starcast does not see them.

Deleting your data

  • From the cloud: sign in, open the account menu (your name at the top right) and choose Delete my data from the cloud. Your saved locations, gear, log and settings are removed from the database immediately.
  • Your sign-in record: to have your account removed entirely, open an issue at github.com/krauskajames/Starcast/issues (without personal details) and it will be deleted. You can also remove Starcast's access at any time from your Google account under Third-party connections.
  • From a device: clear the site data for Starcast in your browser settings.

Children

Starcast is not directed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created by children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be noted in the project's GitHub repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised at github.com/krauskajames/Starcast/issues.
